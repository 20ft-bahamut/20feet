About 20ft

Software Studio & Digital Garage

20ft is a small software studio that builds durable web products, commerce experiences, and Gnuboard 7 extensions. We work in short, focused iterations and ship only what we would use ourselves.

The name comes from the humble length of a standard shipping container — a practical unit that moves real things across the world. We like the idea that software, at its best, is just as reliable and just as unpretentious.
